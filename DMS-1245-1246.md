# DMS-1245 / DMS-1246 Delivery Plan

## Decision Summary

DMS-1245 and DMS-1246 use transactionally recorded, coalesced
`dms.DocumentProjectionWork` as the ordinary completeness inventory for
`dms.DocumentCache`. A supported canonical insert or real `ContentVersion` change and
its current projection requirement commit or roll back together whenever lifecycle is
`Tracking`, `Resetting`, or `Rebuilding`.

The projector pages durable work fairly, materializes the latest coherent document, and
commits a monotonic cache write and matching work acknowledgement in one short
transaction. Startup, restart, steady-state processing, projection operational health,
and caught-up status do not scan the complete canonical/cache relationship.

O(N) source scans are reserved for an explicit existing-database activation baseline,
cache rebuild, or operator-requested integrity scrub. The public Kafka key, value, ETag,
partitioning, compaction, delete, and consumer contracts are unchanged.

## Suggested Implementation Order

1. Deliver DMS-1310 first so durable lifecycle, work, enqueue, access, and provider
   evidence exist. DMS-1311 configuration scaffolding and DMS-1312 materializer work may
   proceed in parallel, but their integrated paths require that schema.
2. Deliver DMS-1313 as the single cache-write/conditional-acknowledgement component, then
   use it from DMS-1314 queue processing and DMS-1315 direct fill.
3. Complete DMS-1314 administration and queue processing before DMS-1316 finalizes
   operational-health/caught-up observations. Qualify the integrated E18 capability in
   DMS-1317; DMS-1318 consumes the queue-capable schema and status evidence.
4. In E19, DMS-1320 provider support and DMS-1322 transform work may proceed in parallel.
   DMS-1321 composes those artifacts into connector templates.
5. DMS-1319 composes the completed E18 observations with provider/connector state.
   DMS-1323 then owns the guarded initial-admission workflow.
6. Complete DMS-1324 contract evidence and DMS-1325 provider E2E scenarios, then verify
   DMS-1326 runbooks against the shipped commands and status surfaces.

[DEPENDENCIES.md](reference/design/backend-redesign/epics/DEPENDENCIES.md). Repository
